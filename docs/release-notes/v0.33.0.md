Released **2026-08-24** · [PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/tesserae/0.33.0/) · [GitHub release](https://github.com/ca1773130n/Tesserae/releases/tag/v0.33.0) · `pip install --upgrade tesserae==0.33.0`

A memory system is judged on what reaches the model, and on how much it cost to
put it there. This release moves both numbers, and then spends most of its
effort establishing which of them could be trusted in the first place.

## The evidence got cheaper

The retrieval budget was being spent twice on the same thing. `documents_of`
collapses hits from one session onto that session, so a ten-hit budget bought a
pooled mean of **6.90 distinct documents** — roughly a third of every prompt was
material already read. `fanout_search` caps hits per source, and asks the
discriminative half of a question separately when the question's own words are
ubiquitous in the corpus.

Then the unit itself changed. Packing whole session documents spent 94.2% of the
prompt on pasted transcript; packing TURNS spends it on the turns a question
actually needs. Measured on LoCoMo conv-26, **43,413 characters became 27,921**
— a 35.7% cut, at better gold-evidence coverage.

The budget-matched control is what makes that a result rather than a saving. The
same session unit given the same 28,000 characters reaches 0.870 coverage where
the pack reaches 0.928, and the control was 1,215 characters under budget when
it lost.

## The answer prompt was the largest single lever

The shipped rule asked for the shortest answer the evidence supports, and
nothing else. `--deliberate` replaces it, and on byte-identical evidence moves
accuracy **from 0.816 to 0.8947 at 7,084 tokens**, against 10,991 before.

Because answer length correlates with judge scores, the length channel was
measured rather than argued. Padding the OLD answers with 11.4 words of
content-free filler bought +0.0197 under the published grader, and **+0.0000**
under a lenient one. The change adds 1.89 words with the median unchanged, which
prices length at 4-7% of the gain, and at zero under the second grader.

## Provenance became a filter, and the filter climbs

`tesserae.vetting` asks whether a source was vetted by someone independent, and
lets a caller filter evidence on it. A filter, never a score: a score blends
vetting, provenance and verdict into one number, and hides the disagreement that
made the question worth asking.

`UNVETTED` and `UNKNOWN` stay distinct from `REJECTED`, because a source with no
record has not been refused — it has not been found. Within one chain the
weakest link governs, since a paragraph is no better vetted than the study it
quotes. Across independent chains they corroborate, so a claim backed by a
reviewed paper and a preprint is stronger than one backed by the preprint alone,
not weaker. And a distilled community summary resolves through `part_of` to the
sources beneath it: distillation may summarise, but it may not launder
provenance.

## Instruments, and what checking them cost

Three benchmarks were examined, and each turned out broken in a different way.
LoCoMo's answer key is 6.4% wrong, so a perfect system scores about 0.936, and
its judge marks **62.81% of deliberately wrong answers CORRECT** when they are
vague but topical. DeepScholar's `cite_p` ranks human-written related work below
the machines reconstructing it. Its `reference_coverage` behaves correctly, and
is now the metric `evals/deepscholar/` targets.

Three bugs of one shape were fixed, and the shape is worth naming: a silent zero
standing in for "not measured". `complete_json` discarded replies it could not
parse — six of twenty-four probed calls, two of them carrying the correct
answer. `_anchor_index` chose a node by type where the answering path decides by
identity, so 33 of 272 session files picked an impostor. And the published
LoCoMo grader was unreachable from this codebase at all, which is why every
report printed `judge: UNMET`.

## `init` no longer overrides your provider

`tesserae init` checked which CLI was installed and wrote that provider into
every new project, overriding the machine-wide config, and pinned a specific
account directory nobody had asked for.
Precedence is now **override, then global config, then detection** — because
detection is a guess and the global config is a decision. `llm_model` and
`llm_codex_home` are inherited from the global layer too.

## Also

- a cross-encoder reranker behind an optional `rerank` extra, its forward pass
  fixed from a 4.97 GB `logits` tensor to 2.4 MB
- `--judge-rules mem0-2026`, a competitor's lenient grader reproduced and
  labelled as lenient in every artifact, so it cannot be quoted as a strict one
- `uv.lock` staleness and a pinned-tokenizer fetch that had left CI red

## An honest note on retrieval

On a 1,552-paper pool, flat BM25 scores 0.541 reference_coverage and this
project's hybrid stack scores 0.518. Four independent measurements this cycle
say the extra lanes dilute BM25 rather than improve it. The arm that would test
the graph itself — compiled concepts and `evidenced_by` edges against the same
control — is built, and not yet run.
